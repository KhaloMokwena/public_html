Using your media
  • Pick it in a slot. Site-wide has the logo, Home has the full-screen background, and About, Services, Careers and Contact each have an optional banner. Open the page in the tabs above and choose from the dropdown.
  • Place it inside text. Type the item's shortcode, such as [media:12], in an About or intro paragraph, a division description or a FAQ answer, and the image appears right there.
  • Attach it to a division. Each About division can show one image, chosen when you edit the division.
  • Gallery. Items with "Show in gallery" ticked appear in the About page gallery. Untick it for logos and backgrounds.
